Deep learning achieves substantial variance reduction over climatology driven by multi-satellite surface dynamic forcing (SST, SSH, SSS, and wind stress curl).

Thermocline Core (100m, 200m)

Error increases sharply near the thermocline core — a known challenge for satellite-trained models, possibly related to sub-grid-scale internal wave activity, though this specific mechanism has not been isolated in this analysis.

Abyssal Regime (700–1000m)

Deep ocean water masses are near-isothermal with tiny seasonal variability (RMSE_climatology ≈ 0.45°C). The neural network's unweighted MSE loss allows residual noise (~0.6–0.8°C), making static climatology mathematically superior at depths below 700m.
