Satellite Remote Sensing

Orbit-to-depth reconstruction

Combining sea surface temperature, salinity, altimetry, and surface winds into deep vertical temperature profiles without requiring in-situ buoys.

A

Satellite-only inputs

No physical sensor drops needed. Sea surface temperature (SST), sea surface height (SSH), sea surface salinity (SSS), ocean currents, and surface winds feed the model directly.

B

Full-depth profile

Standard oceanographic depth layers from surface down to 1000 metres, resolving both mixed layer dynamics and thermocline structure.

C

Continuous basin coverage

Full North Indian Ocean study region covering the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal with 3 years of daily historical data.
